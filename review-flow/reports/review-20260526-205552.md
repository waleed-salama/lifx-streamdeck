### F-02 [P1] [state-management] Active scenes were not canceled when other actions changed the same lights

- Finding ID: `F-02`
- Affected scope: Animated Scene runtime interacting with other LIFX actions
- Package: N/A
- File: `lifx/lifx.go:93`
- Reviewer: Reliability and Quality Engineer

### F-03 [P2] [performance] Scene commands were dispatched serially
